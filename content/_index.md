I’m a DPhil student in AI for the Environment at the University of Oxford. I'm interested in using machine learning to tackle important problems, from understanding our environment to building safe and reliable AI systems. I'm particularly drawn to technical research that could have a large positive impact.

{{< now >}}
I've recently started my DPhil at Oxford, where I'm exploring research directions in environmental machine learning. I'm also increasingly interested in AI safety, particularly how my background in empirical ML and evaluating model failures under extreme conditions could contribute to making advanced AI systems safer. I'm currently taking BlueDot's Technical AI Safety course.

Before Oxford, I completed an MSc in Environmental Data Science and Machine Learning at Imperial College London, where I researched physics-informed deep learning for tropical cyclone wave downscaling. Before that, I studied Geography at the University of Cambridge, where I investigated the limitations of modelling debris-covered glaciers.

Outside of work, I enjoy running (when not injured), being in nature, and building unnecessarily elaborate things in Minecraft.
